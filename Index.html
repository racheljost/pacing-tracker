📡 Sensory load — where are you?

Overwhelm builds gradually. Pick the description that fits right now, even if you feel "fine."

🟢 Regulated
Sounds & lights feel normal. Easy to focus. No urge to escape.
🟡 Building
Slightly irritated by noise or light. Harder to concentrate. Might be moving faster or tensing up without noticing.
🟠 Elevated
Sounds feel too loud. Bright lights bother you. Short fuse. Want to be alone or in a quiet space. Heart rate may feel elevated.
🔴 Overloaded
Hard to process words or instructions. Might be shutting down or melting down. Need to leave the environment now.
